# Raid tactic ledger — what was tried where, what stayed, what was reverted

Started 2026-11-18 (rerun 4) on the owner's word: "We also need to make sure we don't ping pong. At this point, we've
changed things over and over sometimes, likely undoing and redoing the same things. We need to establish the full raid as
a map - such that we know what happens where, the patrols, etc."

This page is the **memory of the raid AI per area**. The geography it refers to (pack ids `P<guid>`, patrol ids, boss
ids, camps) is `RAID_MAP.md` + `raid-map/*.svg` (generated by `tools/raidmap/regen.sh`, never hand-edited). The code
side is `SuiBots/SuiTacticScope.h` (rows) under `RAID_TACTIC_SCOPE_LAW.md`.

## Rules (binding for every agent, rerun 4 on)

1. **Read the area's entry before changing behaviour for it.** If the change you are about to make — or its opposite —
   is already in "Reverted" or "Kept", you need *new* evidence (a fight record, a log line, a guide statement) that the
   earlier entry did not have, and you cite the earlier entry in the new one. No silent re-tries.
2. **One entry per change**, written when it is built: date, week/attempt, the scope row (`SuiTacticScope.h` key) or
   playbook field, what it changes, the evidence, and — filled in later — the **result over N pulls**, not one pull.
   A change is "Kept" only after it has been through at least 3 real pulls of that area without the failure it targets.
3. **Map first, then fix.** A wipe is filed against a map id (`P…`, `B-…`, patrol id, camp). "Somewhere near Geddon" is
   not an address. If the map lacks the thing (a script position, a link), regenerate or extend the map first.
4. **Reliability over progress (owner 11-18).** An area that wipes is drilled to 8 clears in a rolling 10
   (`drill-loop.sh`, `.autopilot drill`) before new tactics elsewhere are added. Bosses are drilled on fresh
   lockouts / encounter resets (QA; loot taken back).
5. **Parameters are data, not ping-pong.** A number that has moved twice (a clearance, a threshold) gets its history
   below; a third move needs a measurement that brackets it (both values tried over ≥ 3 pulls each).

Status words: **ACTIVE** (in the build, gated by a row), **KEPT** (active and proven ≥ 3 pulls), **REVERTED** (taken out
— do not re-add without rule 1), **OPEN** (known failure, no fix yet), **UNSCOPED** (pre-11-15 generic behaviour, still
global — migrate behind a row when its fight is next worked on).
